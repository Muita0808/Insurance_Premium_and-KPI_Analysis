# Insurance Premium and KPI Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Insurance Premium and KPI Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to monitor insurance portfolio performance and support data-driven decision-making. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into premium performance, regional trends, policy maturity, and business growth through dynamic visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

Built for business stakeholders and management teams, the dashboard enables users to explore insurance performance across multiple dimensions, identify growth opportunities, and monitor key financial metrics.

## Business Objectives

* Monitor insurance premium performance across different regions.
* Compare annualized premiums by Zone and Regional Manager.
* Track premium balances to support revenue collection and portfolio management.
* Analyze policies maturing within the next 20–40 years.
* Measure business performance using Return on Investment (ROI) and Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR).
* Enable data-driven decision-making through interactive reporting.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

* Annualized Premium
* Premium Balance
* Return on Investment (ROI)
* Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)
* Policies Maturing in the Next 20–40 Years
* Annualized Premium by Zone
* Annualized Premium by Regional Manager

## Dashboard Features

* Interactive KPI cards providing an executive summary of business performance.
* Regional analysis of annualized premiums by Zone and Regional Manager.
* Premium balance monitoring to track outstanding amounts.
* Long-term policy maturity analysis for policies maturing within the next 20–40 years.
* Dynamic filtering and slicing, allowing users to explore the data by:

  * Policy Type
  * Insurance Agent
  * Customer Occupation
  * Region and Zone
  * Regional Manager
* Cross-filtering visuals that enable users to drill into specific segments and uncover deeper business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* DAX
* Power Query
* Microsoft Excel (Data Preparation)

## Business Value

This dashboard helps decision-makers to:

* Monitor premium performance across regions.
* Identify high-performing and underperforming sales areas.
* Track outstanding premium balances.
* Evaluate long-term policy maturity trends.
* Measure business growth through ROI and CAGR.
* Explore insurance data interactively using multiple business dimensions.
